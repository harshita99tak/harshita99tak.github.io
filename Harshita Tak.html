About

About me

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at Johns Hopkins University. I work on extracellular vesicles (EVs) and their miRNA cargo, using immunocapture of cell-specific EVs (ICE), miRNA profiling, and biomarker development.

My work looks at how cells communicate through small EVs, with applications in neurological, cardiopulmonary and infectious-disease research. I completed my PhD at the Central University of Rajasthan on a UGC Junior Research Fellowship, studying micro-RNAs — mainly miR-181c-5p — in injury and chronic traumatic encephalopathy, then joined Johns Hopkins as a research trainee and postdoctoral fellow.

Research

Research focus

EVs & ICE technology

Immunocapture of cell-specific EVs; isolation and characterization by NanoFCM, tetraspanin/leprechaun and Fluorocet assays; in vivo exosome tracking by IVIS.

miRNA profiling & targets

miRNA library prep and sequencing, digital PCR, and target prediction — miR-181c-5p, miR-133b, miR-34a, miR-146a.

Biomarkers & CTE

EV- and miRNA-based biomarkers, including circulating micro-RNAs in chronic traumatic encephalopathy and other tauopathies.

Computational discovery

Molecular docking and simulation, Argonaute-assisted drug discovery, and pathway analysis (GO, KEGG).
